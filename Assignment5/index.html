Modes of Transportation in the US
This visualization explores transportation patterns across the United States using Census Bureau data. Variables include various modes of transport such as B08301_002E for Car, truck, or van, B08301_003E for driving alone, B08301_004E for Carpool, B08301_010E for Public transportation (excluding taxicab), B08301_011E for Bus, B08301_012E for Subway or elevated rail, B08301_013E for Long-distance train or commuter rail, B08301_015E for Ferryboat, B08301_016E for Taxi, B08301_017E for Motorcycle, B08301_018E for Bicycle, B08301_019E for Walked car/truck usage. The data reveals that in only a few major metropolitan areas do significant numbers of people rely on public transportation, while private vehicles remain the dominant transport mode throughout most of the country.
