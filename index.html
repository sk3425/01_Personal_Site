Transit Travelshed Index in NYC

This map visualizes the Transit Travelshed Index (TTI), representing accessibility and mobility efficiency across different neighborhoods in New York City. Areas with higher TTI values indicate greater congestion and longer travel times, while lower TTI values suggest more efficient transit access.
