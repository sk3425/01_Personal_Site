People Near Bikeways + Public Transport

This map shows the percentage of population living within 300m of protected bicycle infrastructure AND within either 500m of frequent public transport or 1km of mass rapid transport. Globally, only 2% of people in urban areas have access to both.

Legend: Step-Based Classification (Albers Projection)

Major Transit Hubs

20-35%: Moderate Access
35-50%: Better Access
Above 60%: Excellent Access
